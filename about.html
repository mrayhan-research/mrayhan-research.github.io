About

Independent research in AI systems.

This is the public research home of Muhammad Rayhan. The program investigates how AI systems can be evaluated, secured, and made more accountable in practice.

Its long-term aim is to turn credible research into collaboration, community, and technology that deserves to exist. The standard is deliberately demanding: a useful result must survive attempts to refute it.

For current work and future release bundles, visit the GitHub organization.

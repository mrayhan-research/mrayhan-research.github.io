Research standard

01

Evidence before claims

Public conclusions must map to inspectable material, not merely persuasive language.

02

Negative results count

A result that rules out a bad instrument or weak question is progress when the record is clear.

03

Limits stay visible

Every release states what it establishes, what it does not establish, and what remains open.

Open research

A release is an evidence package.

Code and data are useful only when a reader can identify what they support, reproduce the relevant result, and see the limits of the claim.

  1. Evidence: every public conclusion has a checkable source or execution record.
  2. Rights: public bundles redistribute only material the project may lawfully share.
  3. Safety: releases exclude credentials, personal data, private paths, and unreviewed attack-enabling details.
  4. Independent check: another reviewer verifies the exact public release.
  5. Correction: errors are dated and corrected openly; files are versioned rather than silently replaced.

Cleared release bundles will appear here and in the organization's GitHub repositories.
